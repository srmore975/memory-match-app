Privacy Policy — Memory Match! Cartoon Fun

Last updated: September 7, 2026

1. Overview

Memory Match! Cartoon Fun ("the App") is a single-player memory matching game designed for children. This policy explains, in simple terms, what information the App collects and how it is handled.

The short version: the game itself never collects personal information, and everything the game remembers stays on your child's device. The only outside service is Google AdMob, which serves family-safe, non-personalized ads to keep the App free (details in section 3).

2. Information we collect

The App collects no personal information whatsoever. Specifically, the App does NOT collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Photos, videos, audio, or contacts
  • Health, financial, or payment information
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or tracking cookies
  • Any data you type or generate beyond the game itself

Data stored locally on the device

The App saves a small amount of game progress locally on your device so it is not lost between sessions:

  • Coin balance earned by playing
  • Unlocked stickers
  • Best scores and time for each theme and difficulty
  • A simple count of games played and games won

This data is stored only on the device using the device's standard local web storage. It is never transmitted to us or to any server, and it is never shared with anyone.

Technical requests

  • The App requests standard fonts (Google Fonts) from Google's servers to render its cartoon look. As with any web request, Google may briefly see the request's network information (such as IP address). No gameplay data is sent.
  • The App uses the device's built-in text-to-speech feature for spoken encouragement. This runs entirely on the device; no audio is recorded or uploaded.

3. Third parties (advertising)

The App displays ads from Google AdMob (a service of Google LLC) to help keep the App free:

  • What the ad service sees: when an ad is shown, Google's ad SDK may receive the device's advertising identifier, network information (such as IP address), and anonymous ad impression/click data. This flows between your device and Google under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Children's ads: this App is built for families. Ads are served as non-personalized requests with a maximum content rating of "T" (Teen) or lower, and the ad SDK is configured with child-directed treatment. No interest-based or behavioral advertising is used.
  • Ad formats: a banner ad at the top of some screens, and a full-screen ad occasionally after you finish a game.
  • Opting out (for parents): you can delete the App at any time, or disable advertising personalization in device settings (Ads → opt out of personalization).

The App itself contains no other third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising services beyond AdMob.

4. Permissions

The App requests no sensitive device permissions (no camera, microphone, contacts, location, or storage access).

5. Children's privacy

The App complies with child-privacy laws including the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation for children (GDPR-K):

  • No personal information is collected from children.
  • Only the Google AdMob ad SDK runs in the App, serving non-personalized, child-directed ads (see section 3). Personalized/behavioral advertising is disabled.
  • No social features, chat, or user-generated content exist in the App.
  • There is no way for a child to contact others or share content outside the App.

Because the App itself collects no personal data and ads are non-personalized, there is nothing to retain, and no consent is required for data processing.

6. Deleting data

Parents can delete the local game data at any time:

  • In the App: open Parents (bottom navigation) → Reset Data; or
  • Uninstall the App, which removes all locally stored data; or
  • Clear the App or site data for the App in the device settings.

7. Security

The little data the App writes is stored locally on the device and is protected by the device's own security. Since no data leaves the device, there is no risk of it being intercepted in transit.

8. Changes to this policy

If we ever change the way the App handles data, we will update this policy and the effective date above. If the App ever begins collecting personal information, this policy will be updated accordingly and require steps consistent with applicable child-privacy law.
